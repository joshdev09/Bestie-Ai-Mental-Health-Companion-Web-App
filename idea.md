## **Bestie - Mental Health Companion App**



### **Core Features**



###### **Emotional Check-ins**

 	- Daily mood tracking with gentle prompts.

 	- Journaling space for thoughts and feelings.

 	- Streak tracking to build healthy habits.



###### **Conversational AI Companion**

 	- Natural, empathetic chat interface.

 	- Remembers previous conversations and user preferences.

 	- Adaptive tone based on user's emotional state.

 	- Topic suggestions when users don't know what to talk about.



###### **Activities \& Coping Tools**

 	- Guided breathing exercises.

 	- Grounding techniques for anxiety.

 	- Positive affirmation library.

 	- Creative prompts (drawing, writing, music suggestions).



###### **Connection Features**

 	- Anonymous peer support groups by interest/struggle.

 	- "Letter to yourself" time capsules.

 	- Gratitude journal prompts.

 	- Celebrate small wins together.
